Cutroom for Flow

Open Google Flow in this window, then come back here.

1. Storyboard

Paste the storyboard from Cutroom (Script step → Storyboard → Copy all), or load its .txt. Any list of numbered prompts works too.

2. Your character

One to three pictures of your main character. They go with every scene that shows a person, so the character looks the same throughout.

3. Flow's controls

Cutroom looks for Flow's prompt box and buttons by itself. If it picks the wrong one, show it each one once.
