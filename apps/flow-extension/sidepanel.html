From Cutroom:

Each picture goes straight onto its scene in Cutroom as soon as it's made. Keep a Cutroom tab open.

Open a project in Cutroom (any step) and it shows up here, ready to make its pictures. Or press Make in Flow on its Images step. Every picture goes back onto its scene.

Or paste a storyboard (Script step → Storyboard → Copy all), or load its .txt. Pictures then go to Downloads.

Using Cutroom online?

Cutroom on this computer (localhost) works as it is. For Cutroom online, enter its address once and allow it: its projects then get the Make in Flow button too.

2. Your character

One to three pictures of your main character. They go with every scene that shows a person, so the character looks the same throughout.

3. Flow's controls
